About Us

Pints & Pals was founded in May 2025 by Al, Kyle, and Hugh with the goal of creating a great D&D game open to anyone with a community of players at its heart. From that Pints & Pals has gone on to grown to multiple weekly games and monthly community days but still with that same love for the TTRPG community at the heart of it.
